How the FIFO rule works

Place a third symbol without completing a winning line, and your oldest placed symbol begins to breathe — a soft scale animation warning you it's about to go. On your next move, it's removed. Each player is capped at three symbols on the board at once, so the game never freezes into a dead draw — it just keeps moving.
